Acoustic Engineering

Designed for Audiophile Tactility

Engineered from first principles with real-time digital signal processing and zero-compromise design.

3D Binaural HRTF Soundstage

Immersive 3D audio positioning via Web Audio API PannerNode. Keys are mathematically projected across physical stereo and vertical elevation coordinates.

100% Privacy & Zero Tracking

Your keystrokes and typing data never leave your device. Zero telemetry, no background analytics, and no network tracking.

Zero-Latency Native Hooks

Backed by low-level native hooks (uiohook-napi) in the desktop app, ensuring immediate acoustic feedback across all games, IDEs, and office apps.

Stealth Mode & Tray Integration

Minimizes silently to the Windows System Tray with ultra-low hardware footprint. Toggle global instant mute anytime with Ctrl+Shift+M.

Free & 100% Open Source

Fully transparent, community-driven, and licensed under the permissive MIT License. Free to inspect, customize, and build custom soundpacks.

Cute & Friendly Rounded Aesthetic

Warm linen, alabaster porcelain, and royal bordeaux palette with cute rounded Quicksand & Nunito typography and seamless dark & light theme modes.
